# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Drivers use the delivery-execution workspace on the road to review assigned stops, capture delivery evidence, and report incidents. Dispatchers and administrators use the operational workspace to locate orders and inspect their private evidence.

## Product Purpose

ERP Logistic supports last-mile delivery operations in Lima and Callao. Phase 4 helps drivers record each order's outcome with retained photo evidence and gives authorized staff a tenant-scoped way to inspect that evidence. Success means an operator can trust that a recorded delivery or incident has its required evidence and that private photos are only shown to authorized users.

## Brand Personality

Precise, reliable, and direct. The interface should support clear operational decisions without obscuring delivery status or evidence availability.

## Anti-references

Manifest is a visual and UX reference only. Do not reuse its code, assets, or brand identity. No other specific visual anti-reference has been provided.

## Design Principles

- Make the next operational action and each order's current outcome easy to identify.
- Keep evidence private, retained, and visibly distinguishable from a local preview or an incomplete upload.
- Give drivers and administrators role-appropriate views and actions.
- Explain errors and empty states with a concrete next step.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No additional user-specific accessibility requirements have been provided. Preserve keyboard access, clear focus indication, responsive layouts, readable contrast, and reduced-motion support.
