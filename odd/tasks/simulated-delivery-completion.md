## Specs

### S1 — Complete simulated route at destination
- Authorized scope: simulated GPS arrivals may complete their assigned simulated delivery and route without real photo evidence, while remaining explicitly auditable as simulation; ordinary driver confirmation keeps its current evidence and actor requirements.
- User requirement: “pero la simulación debe marcar como culminada la carrera si ya llego al punto”
- User authorization: “si por favor” — in response to authorizing a simulation-only outcome with no real photo and clearly audited provenance.
- Acceptance: an arrived simulated one-stop, one-order route records a terminal delivered attempt/order and completed route with explicit simulation provenance; non-simulated execution still requires ordinary evidence; repeated ticks cannot duplicate completion. Route completion is committed only after a simulated position is recorded at the delivery destination. Multi-stop and multi-order routes are not auto-completed by this single-destination flow.
